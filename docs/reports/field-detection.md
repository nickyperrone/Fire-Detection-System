# Field detection benchmark

Generated by `make field-detection-benchmark` on 2026-10-04. Spec: [10-field-detection](../10-field-detection.md).

- Parcels sampled: 90 rural parcels of 20–400 ha from the cadastres in the database. 40 are one field (at least 80% of the parcel has the same crop history); the rest hold several fields, and an outline cannot be scored against them.
- Score: IoU between the outline found from a tap at the parcel's representative point and the parcel. 1 is a perfect match; 0.7 or more is an outline that only needs its corners checked.
- "No field found" is an honest failure: the app asks to draw by hand.
- Time: 0.03 s to find an outline once the images are read; 11 s (median of 6) to read a place's year of images the first time.
